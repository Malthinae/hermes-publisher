Privacy Policy

Hermes desktop application · last updated 22 October 2026

This policy covers the Hermes desktop application (the "app"), a personal publishing tool that renders short vertical videos and uploads them to its operator's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

Who this covers

The app has exactly one user: the owner of the computer it runs on and of the TikTok account it is authorised for. It is not a service offered to the public, it has no sign-up, and it collects nothing from anyone else.

What data the app handles

  • TikTok authorisation data: an access token and a rotating refresh token, plus the basic profile fields returned by user.info.basic (open ID, display name, avatar URL) for the single authorised account.
  • Content created by the app: video files, scripts, voice-over audio, captions and thumbnails, all produced locally from the operator's own inputs.
  • Publish results returned by TikTok: video IDs, publish status and error messages, stored so failed uploads can be retried.

Where the data goes

Tokens and configuration are stored on the operator's own computer, in the app's local environment file inside the operator's user profile. Videos and publish records stay on the same machine. The only network destination is TikTok's official API, over HTTPS, plus optional read-only public endpoints used to research topics.

The app does not sell data, does not share it with advertisers or analytics providers, contains no tracking SDKs, and does not read any other account's data.

Retention

Tokens are kept until they are revoked or the app is uninstalled. Videos, captions and publish records are kept until the operator deletes them.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Revoking access invalidates the tokens immediately. Local data is removed by deleting the app's folder from the computer.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not offered to any other person.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version replaces this page.
